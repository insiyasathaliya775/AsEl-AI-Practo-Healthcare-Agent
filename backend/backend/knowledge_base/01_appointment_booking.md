
# Appointment Booking Policy

Patients can request appointments in General Medicine, Cardiology, Dermatology, Pediatrics, and Orthopedics. Each appointment is assigned a unique appointment ID and a status such as Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, No-Show, or Rescheduled. Appointment availability and confirmation must be checked through the relevant booking system. This document describes a fictional policy for the AsEl-AI Practo healthcare support capstone.
