# Consultation fees

Consultation fees vary by specialty and are displayed in Indian rupees before booking. For this synthetic project, General Medicine fees range from ₹300–₹700, Cardiology from ₹600–₹1,500, Dermatology from ₹400–₹1,000, Pediatrics from ₹350–₹800, and Orthopedics from ₹500–₹1,200. The actual fee for a record should be retrieved from the appointment dataset.
